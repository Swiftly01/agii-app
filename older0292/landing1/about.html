About Us

Agii.ng is Nigeria’s premier platform connecting customers with reliable service providers across various sectors. Whether you're looking for a skilled artisan, a handyman, or a trusted manufacturer, Agii.ng simplifies the process of finding the best service provider in your area.

What Makes Agii.ng Unique?

Hyperlocal Connections

Our platform uses advanced location technology to connect you with service providers just 10 meters away, ensuring timely and efficient services.

Seamless Google Maps Integration

Navigate easily with built-in Google Maps functionality, allowing you to reach vendors and service providers effortlessly.

User-Friendly Interface

Agii.ng provides a streamlined platform where users can easily browse, compare, and choose the best service providers for their needs.

Benefits for Users

  • Effortless Discovery: Find reliable service providers quickly and efficiently.
  • Time & Cost Efficiency: Save time and reduce costs with local providers.
  • Transparent Information: Access detailed profiles, pricing, and reviews for informed decisions.
  • Easy Navigation: Use integrated Google Maps for easy discovery of service providers.
  • Safety & Trust: Engage with verified, trusted vendors only.
  • Enhanced Brand Exposure: Showcase your services to a wider audience.
  • Cost-Effective Advertising: Reach customers without the high costs of traditional advertising.
  • Direct Customer Interaction: Communicate with customers directly for better engagement.
  • Location-Based Advantage: Improve visibility with Google Maps integration for better customer reach.
  • Increased Credibility: Build customer trust through our verification system.

Our Mission

We aim to bridge the gap between customers and service providers by creating a platform that is both efficient and reliable.

Our Vision

To be the leading platform connecting users with trusted service providers, empowering local businesses, and enhancing customer satisfaction.
